# Syllabus (same topics for every language)

## Level 1 (like grade 1–2 English listening tests)

Short everyday sentences, present tense, the most common words. Each lesson has about 12–13 sentences and is about 4 minutes long with the repeat.

| lesson | topic | key words / patterns (German) |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Hello! | hallo, ich heiße…, wie geht es dir?, wie alt bist du? |
| 02 | Colors | rot, blau, grün, gelb, schwarz, weiß; Lieblingsfarbe |
| 03 | Numbers 1–10 | eins … zehn; wie viele…?; ich habe… |
| 04 | My family | Vater, Mutter, Bruder, Schwester, Opa, Oma; wer ist das? |
| 05 | My body | Kopf, Augen, Nase, Mund, Ohren, Hand, Fuß; commands (berühre, heb, klatsch) |
| 06 | Animals | Hund, Katze, Affe, Panda, Tiger, Maus, Ente, Vogel; kann schwimmen/fliegen |
| 07 | Food and drinks | Hunger, Durst, Milch, Reis, Fisch, Nudeln, Wasser; ich möchte… |
| 08 | At school | Lineal, Bleistift, Radiergummi, Buch, Schultasche; classroom commands |
| 09 | Fruit | Apfel, Banane, Orange, Zitrone, Birne, Trauben; süß/sauer |
| 10 | Toys | Auto, Teddybär, Puppe, Ball, Drachen; wo ist…? auf/unter/in |
| 11 | Weather | sonnig, bewölkt, regnet, schneit, windig, heiß, kalt |
| 12 | Clothes | Hut, Kleid, Schuhe, Hemd, Socken, Jacke, Hose; anziehen/ausziehen |

## Level 2 (like grade 3–4)

Times, days, places, can/can't, prices, simple directions, and at the end the simple past. Each lesson has 13 sentences and is about 4 minutes long.

| lesson | topic | patterns |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Time and my day | wie spät ist es? um … Uhr / 几点？…点半 |
| 02 | Days of the week | Montag … Sonntag / 星期一 … 星期天 |
| 03 | My home | Zimmer, Küche, neben / 客厅, 厨房, 旁边 |
| 04 | Hobbies | ich kann…, gern / 会, 喜欢 |
| 05 | Seasons and months | Frühling…Winter, Januar… / 春夏秋冬, 一月… |
| 06 | Shopping | was kostet…? Euro / 多少钱？块, 斤 |
| 07 | At a restaurant | ich hätte gern…, die Rechnung / 我要…, 买单 |
| 08 | Jobs | von Beruf, werden / 做什么工作, 当 |
| 09 | Getting around | mit dem Bus, links/rechts / 坐, 骑, 往左拐 |
| 10 | Birthdays and dates | am dritten Mai / 几月几号 |
| 11 | Feelings and health | müde, Kopfschmerzen / 累, 头疼, 感冒 |
| 12 | Yesterday | Perfekt (habe … gemacht) / 了 |

## Level 3 ideas (later)
Weather forecast and plans (will/going to), comparing (bigger, faster), the city and directions, school subjects and timetable, food and cooking, travel and holidays, telephone calls, the past in longer stories, nature and animals, sports, festivals, health and the body.
