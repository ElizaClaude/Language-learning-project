# Level 1, Lesson 7: Food and drinks
# one sentence per line:  German | English
Ich habe Hunger. | I'm hungry.
Ich habe Durst. | I'm thirsty.
Möchtest du Milch? | Would you like some milk?
Ja, bitte. | Yes, please.
Nein, danke. | No, thank you.
Was möchtest du essen? | What would you like to eat?
Ich möchte Nudeln. | I'd like some noodles.
Ich mag Reis und Fisch. | I like rice and fish.
Ich mag keine Karotten. | I don't like carrots.
Kann ich Wasser haben? | Can I have some water?
Ein Ei und einen Saft, bitte. | An egg and a juice, please.
Das Brot ist lecker. | The bread is delicious.
Guten Appetit! | Enjoy your meal!
