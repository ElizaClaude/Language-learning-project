# Level 1, Lesson 5: My body
# one sentence per line:  Spanish (Spain) | English
Esta es mi cabeza. | This is my head.
Tengo dos ojos. | I have two eyes.
Tengo una nariz y una boca. | I have a nose and a mouth.
Tócate las orejas. | Touch your ears.
Levanta la mano. | Raise your hand.
¡Aplaude! | Clap your hands!
¡Pisa fuerte! | Stamp your feet!
Cierra los ojos. | Close your eyes.
Abre la boca. | Open your mouth.
Lávate las manos. | Wash your hands.
Me duele la pierna. | My leg hurts.
El elefante tiene orejas grandes. | The elephant has big ears.
El conejo tiene orejas largas. | The rabbit has long ears.
