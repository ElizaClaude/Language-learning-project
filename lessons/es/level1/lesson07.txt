# Level 1, Lesson 7: Food and drinks
# one sentence per line:  Spanish (Spain) | English
Tengo hambre. | I'm hungry.
Tengo sed. | I'm thirsty.
¿Quieres leche? | Would you like some milk?
Sí, por favor. | Yes, please.
No, gracias. | No, thank you.
¿Qué quieres comer? | What would you like to eat?
Quiero un bocadillo. | I'd like a sandwich.
Me gusta el arroz con pescado. | I like rice with fish.
No me gustan las zanahorias. | I don't like carrots.
¿Me das agua, por favor? | Can I have some water, please?
Un huevo y un zumo, por favor. | An egg and a juice, please.
El pan está delicioso. | The bread is delicious.
¡Buen provecho! | Enjoy your meal!
