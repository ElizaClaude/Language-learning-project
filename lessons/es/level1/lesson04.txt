# Level 1, Lesson 4: My family
# one sentence per line:  Spanish (Spain) | English
Esta es mi familia. | This is my family.
Este es mi papá. | This is my dad.
Esta es mi mamá. | This is my mom.
¿Quién es? | Who is this?
Este es mi hermano. | This is my brother.
Esta es mi hermana. | This is my sister.
Mi abuelo es mayor. | My grandpa is old.
A mi abuela le gusta cocinar. | My grandma likes to cook.
¿Tienes un hermano? | Do you have a brother?
No, pero tengo una hermana. | No, but I have a sister.
Mi papá es médico. | My father is a doctor.
Mi mamá es maestra. | My mother is a teacher.
Quiero mucho a mi familia. | I love my family.
