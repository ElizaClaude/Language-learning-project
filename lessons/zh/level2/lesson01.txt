# Level 2, Lesson 1: Time and my day
# one sentence per line:  Chinese | English
现在几点了？ | What time is it now?
现在七点。 | It's seven o'clock.
现在七点半。 | It's half past seven.
我七点起床。 | I get up at seven.
我七点半吃早饭。 | I have breakfast at seven thirty.
我八点去上学。 | I go to school at eight.
八点一刻上课。 | Class starts at a quarter past eight.
我十二点吃午饭。 | I have lunch at twelve.
我四点回家。 | I come home at four.
然后我做作业。 | Then I do my homework.
我们六点吃晚饭。 | We have dinner at six.
我九点睡觉。 | I go to bed at nine.
快点儿，要迟到了！ | Hurry up, we're going to be late!
