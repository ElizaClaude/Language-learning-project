# Level 2, Lesson 6: Shopping
# one sentence per line:  Chinese | English
这个多少钱？ | How much is this?
两块钱。 | It's two yuan.
我想要这件T恤。 | I'd like this T-shirt.
你要多大号的？ | What size do you want?
有蓝色的吗？ | Do you have it in blue?
太贵了。 | It's too expensive.
很便宜。 | It's cheap.
我买了。 | I'll take it.
苹果多少钱一斤？ | How much are the apples per half kilo?
三块钱一斤。 | Three yuan per half kilo.
给你钱。 | Here's the money.
找你五块。 | Here's five yuan change.
谢谢，再见！ | Thank you, goodbye!
