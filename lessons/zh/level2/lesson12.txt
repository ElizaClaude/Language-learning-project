# one sentence per line:  Chinese | English

我们去动物园了。 | We went to the zoo.
我去看奶奶了。 | I visited my grandma.
我们吃了面条。 | We ate noodles.

很有意思。 | It was very interesting.

我去公园了。 | I went to the park.
天气很好。 | The weather was nice.
你做完作业了吗？ | Did you finish your homework?
做完了！ | Yes, I'm done!
