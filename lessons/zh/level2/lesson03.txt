# Level 2, Lesson 3: My home
# one sentence per line:  Chinese | English
这是我的家。 | This is my home.
我家有三个房间。 | My home has three rooms.
这是客厅。 | This is the living room.
厨房很大。 | The kitchen is big.
我的房间很小，但是很漂亮。 | My room is small but nice.
洗手间在哪儿？ | Where is the bathroom?
在厨房旁边。 | It's next to the kitchen.
妈妈在厨房里。 | Mom is in the kitchen.
爸爸在客厅看电视。 | Dad is watching TV in the living room.
猫在沙发上睡觉。 | The cat is sleeping on the sofa.
卧室里有一张床。 | There is a bed in the bedroom.
有一个窗户和一扇门。 | There is a window and a door.
请进！ | Come in!
