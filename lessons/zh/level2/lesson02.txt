# Level 2, Lesson 2: Days of the week
# one sentence per line:  Chinese | English

明天星期二。 | Tomorrow is Tuesday.
星期三我有音乐课。 | On Wednesday I have music class.
星期四我踢足球。 | On Thursday I play soccer.
我最喜欢星期五。 | Friday is my favorite day.
星期六我去看奶奶。 | On Saturday I visit my grandma.
星期天我睡懒觉。 | On Sunday I sleep in.
你周末做什么？ | What do you do on the weekend?
我去公园。 | I go to the park.
一个星期有七天。 | A week has seven days.
星期一你有什么课？ | What classes do you have on Monday?
