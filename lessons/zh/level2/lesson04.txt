# Level 2, Lesson 4: Hobbies
# one sentence per line:  Chinese | English
你的爱好是什么？ | What's your hobby?
我喜欢看书。 | I like reading.
我喜欢画画。 | I like drawing.
你会游泳吗？ | Can you swim?
会，我会游泳。 | Yes, I can swim.
不会，我不会游泳。 | No, I can't swim.
我会弹钢琴。 | I can play the piano.
我哥哥喜欢打乒乓球。 | My older brother likes playing ping-pong.
她唱歌唱得很好。 | She sings very well.
我们喜欢跳舞。 | We like dancing.
你会骑自行车吗？ | Can you ride a bike?
我们一起打篮球吧？ | Shall we play basketball together?
好主意！ | Good idea!
