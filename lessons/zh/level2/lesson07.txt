# Level 2, Lesson 7: At a restaurant
# one sentence per line:  Chinese | English
我很饿。 | I'm very hungry.
我们去饭馆吧！ | Let's go to a restaurant!
你们想点什么？ | What would you like to order?
我要一碗汤。 | I'd like a bowl of soup.
我要鸡肉和米饭。 | I'd like chicken and rice.
你们想喝点什么？ | Would you like something to drink?
请给我一杯橙汁。 | A glass of orange juice, please.
真好吃！ | This is delicious!
我吃饱了。 | I'm full.
服务员，买单！ | Waiter, the check please!
一共二十块。 | That's twenty yuan in total.
我会用筷子。 | I can use chopsticks.
请给我一把勺子。 | A spoon, please.
