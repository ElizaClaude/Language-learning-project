# Level 1, Lesson 5: My body
# one sentence per line:  Chinese | English
这是我的头。 | This is my head.
我有两只眼睛。 | I have two eyes.
我有一个鼻子和一张嘴。 | I have a nose and a mouth.
摸摸你的耳朵。 | Touch your ears.
举起你的手。 | Raise your hand.
拍拍手！ | Clap your hands!
跺跺脚！ | Stamp your feet!
闭上眼睛。 | Close your eyes.
张开嘴巴。 | Open your mouth.
去洗手。 | Go wash your hands.
我的腿疼。 | My leg hurts.
大象的耳朵很大。 | The elephant has big ears.
兔子的耳朵很长。 | The rabbit has long ears.
