# Level 1, Lesson 3: Numbers 1-10
# one sentence per line:  Chinese | English
一，二，三，四，五。 | One, two, three, four, five.
六，七，八，九，十。 | Six, seven, eight, nine, ten.
跟我一起数！ | Count with me!
你有几本书？ | How many books do you have?
我有三本书。 | I have three books.
你看到几只鸟？ | How many birds can you see?
我看到四只鸟。 | I can see four birds.
我有两只手。 | I have two hands.
这里有五支铅笔。 | Here are five pencils.
你妹妹几岁？ | How old is your little sister?
她九岁。 | She's nine.
请给我十个苹果。 | Ten apples, please.
