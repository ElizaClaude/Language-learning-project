# Level 1, Lesson 11: Weather
# one sentence per line:  Chinese | English
今天天气怎么样？ | What's the weather like today?
今天是晴天。 | It's sunny.
今天是阴天。 | It's cloudy.
下雨了。 | It's raining.
下雪了！ | It's snowing!
今天风很大。 | It's windy.
天气很热。 | It's hot.
天气很冷。 | It's cold.
北京暖和吗？ | Is it warm in Beijing?
是的，很暖和。 | Yes, it's warm.
带上你的雨伞。 | Take your umbrella.
我们堆雪人吧！ | Let's make a snowman!
