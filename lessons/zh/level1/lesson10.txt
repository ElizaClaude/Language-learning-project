# Level 1, Lesson 10: Toys
# one sentence per line:  Chinese | English

我的娃娃很漂亮。 | My doll is beautiful.

在桌子上。 | It's on the table.

我的风筝飞得很高。 | My kite flies high.
我可以和你一起玩吗？ | May I play with you?
当然可以！ | Yes, of course!
我们一起玩吧！ | Let's play!
把你的玩具收好。 | Put away your toys.
